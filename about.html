About

Hi, I’m Cliff Stornell.

A counsellor, a fellow traveller, and a firm believer that no one should have to navigate the hard chapters of life alone.

My philosophy

You are the author of your own story.

I believe people are not broken and don’t need fixing. We all carry stories — about who we are, what we’re capable of, and what we deserve. Sometimes those stories, shaped by hard experiences, stop serving us. Therapy is a chance to notice them, question the ones that no longer fit, and re-author a narrative that reflects who you truly are and where you want to go.

My style is warm, collaborative, and down-to-earth. I’ll meet you with curiosity rather than assumptions, and we’ll move at a pace that feels safe. You bring the expertise on your own life; I bring tools, questions, and a steady presence to help you find the way forward.
