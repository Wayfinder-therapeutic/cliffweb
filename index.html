What therapy is like here

A conversation, not a diagnosis to fix.

Starting therapy can feel like standing at a trailhead without a map. My job isn’t to hand you directions — it’s to walk alongside you while you discover the way that’s right for your life.

Sessions are collaborative and unhurried. We’ll talk about what’s weighing on you, notice the stories you’ve been telling about yourself, and gently explore new ones. There’s no script, no judgment — just a steady, warm space to think out loud.

Rooted in narrative therapy

“You are not the problem. The problem is the problem — and the story you live can be re-authored.”

Much of my work draws on narrative therapy: the idea that we make sense of our lives through stories, and that those stories can be revisited, questioned, and rewritten. Together we’ll separate you from the problems you’re facing and reconnect with your own values, strengths, and preferred way forward.
