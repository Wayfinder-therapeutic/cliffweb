Services

Ways we can work together.

Whatever chapter you’re in, there’s a way forward. Explore the kinds of therapy I offer and the approaches that guide our work — all available in person in Winnipeg or by secure telehealth.

Types of therapy

Who I work with

Individual therapy

A dedicated space for you. We’ll work through anxiety, depression, stress, grief, self-worth, identity, life transitions, and the search for direction and meaning.

Couples & relationship therapy

Support for partners of every kind — married, dating, long-distance, polyamorous, or navigating change. Strengthen communication, rebuild trust, and reconnect.

Group therapy

Facilitated groups where you can share the path with others who understand. Connection, perspective, and the reminder that you’re not walking alone.

Approaches & modalities

The tools I bring to our work

Therapy isn’t one-size-fits-all. I draw on a range of evidence-informed approaches and tailor our work to what fits you best.

Narrative therapy

The heart of my practice. We separate you from your problems and explore the stories you live by — so you can re-author a narrative aligned with your values and hopes.

Person-centred & humanistic

You lead. I offer genuine warmth, empathy, and unconditional positive regard, trusting in your own capacity to grow and heal.

Cognitive & behavioural tools

Practical strategies to notice unhelpful thought patterns, ease anxiety and low mood, and build skills you can carry into everyday life.

Conflict resolution

Specialized training to help individuals, couples, and groups move through tension and disagreement toward mutual understanding.

Solution-focused

When it fits, we’ll build on what’s already working and set clear, meaningful goals for the road ahead.

Affirming & identity-informed

Care that centres your identity — gender, sexuality, relationship style, neurodivergence, and culture — as strengths, never things to be fixed.
